# Sprout Streak Brand Concepts

These exploratory raster concepts were generated from the product direction in
`docs/BUSINESS_REQUIREMENTS.md` and `docs/TECHNICAL_REQUIREMENTS.md`.

The shared design brief was:

- serve K-8 students without looking preschool-only;
- earn the trust of teachers, parents, and schools;
- communicate real money habits rather than generic points or rewards;
- support the product's classroom-to-home continuity story;
- stay clear, high-contrast, and recognizable at small sizes;
- avoid advertising, banking, investment, and stock-classroom visual tropes.

These are concept-selection assets, not final production artwork. The selected
direction should be redrawn as true vector artwork, its typography licensed and
manually typeset, and its colors checked against WCAG contrast targets before it
is shipped.

`comparison-sheet.png` presents all nine assets together for quick review.

## Concept 1: Growing Balance

The clearest, warmest interpretation of the name: a sprout rises from a coin,
and the larger leaf carries a subtle upward-progress gesture. It immediately
connects money with growth, although it is also the closest to familiar
financial-literacy imagery.

Palette: deep evergreen, fresh mint, warm golden yellow, warm cream.

- `logo.png` — transparent horizontal lockup
- `icon.png` — square app-icon treatment
- `banner.png` — wide growth-sequence banner

Prompt set:

- Logo: Original horizontal "Sprout Streak" logo; a simple sprout emerging from
  a round coin, two leaves subtly forming upward progress; clean vector-friendly
  rounded educational-tech style; friendly to children and credible to adults;
  evergreen, mint, and gold; transparent background; exact name once; no slogan,
  dollar signs, piggy bank, mascot, people, mockup, or watermark.
- Icon: Distill the same sprout-and-coin symbol into a bold square app icon on a
  warm-cream field; retain evergreen, mint, and gold; no text; remain legible at
  32 pixels.
- Banner: A wide cream banner showing golden coin-seeds progressing into mint
  sprouts, ending in a healthy two-leaf sprout; include the exact logo once;
  spacious, responsive-safe, vector-like, and free of extra copy.

## Concept 2: One Path

The most differentiated product story: one continuous path connects school and
home, with a leaf at the midpoint. It makes the classroom/family continuity
promise visible without reducing the product to a generic money app.

Palette: deep navy, bright teal, coral accent, pale cool blue.

- `logo.png` — transparent horizontal lockup
- `icon.png` — square app-icon treatment
- `banner.png` — wide classroom-to-home continuity banner

Prompt set:

- Logo: Original horizontal "Sprout Streak" logo; a smooth S-shaped path linking
  simplified school and home endpoints with a leaf at the midpoint; welcoming,
  collaborative educational-tech geometry; navy, teal, and coral; transparent
  background; exact name once; no money symbols, coins, piggy bank, mascot,
  people, slogan, mockup, or watermark.
- Icon: Distill the same S-path, school endpoint, home endpoint, and paired leaf
  into a bold square app icon on a pale cool-blue field; no text; preserve the
  navy, teal, and coral identity; remain legible at 32 pixels.
- Banner: A wide pale-blue banner with one teal path traveling from a simplified
  school context to a home context through leaf milestones; exact logo once;
  generous crop-safe space and no extra copy.

## Concept 3: Progress Pulse

The most energetic and tween-friendly direction: a rhythmic sequence of small
seed-like actions compounds into a sprout. It communicates a streak directly
and avoids literal banking iconography.

Palette: deep indigo, chartreuse-lime, warm apricot, pale lavender-white.

- `logo.png` — transparent horizontal lockup
- `icon.png` — square app-icon treatment
- `banner.png` — wide repeated-progress banner

Prompt set:

- Logo: Original horizontal "Sprout Streak" logo; four rounded seed-like shapes
  rise in rhythm and the last opens into a two-leaf sprout; bold modular
  educational-tech geometry; indigo, chartreuse-lime, and apricot; transparent
  background; exact name once; no currency symbols, coins, arrows, piggy bank,
  mascot, people, slogan, mockup, or watermark.
- Icon: Tighten the same four-part progression into a bold square app icon on a
  deep-indigo field, moving from apricot to chartreuse-lime; no text; remain
  legible at 32 pixels.
- Banner: A wide lavender-white banner with repeated rounded milestones growing
  toward a large two-leaf sprout; exact logo once; energetic, spacious,
  responsive-safe, and free of extra copy.

## Generation

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. The logo PNGs include
alpha transparency; icons and banners intentionally use solid backgrounds.
